Restore or manage WatchWallet Pro

To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open Settings → Upgrade to WatchWallet Pro in WatchWallet and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. To cancel a subscription, open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

Delete my data

Your data is only on your iPhone. Delete single expenses in Activity, or everything at once with Settings → Erase All Data. Deleting the app also deletes its data (copies in your iPhone or iCloud backups are managed by Apple).

Change the language

WatchWallet is available in English, Russian and Kazakh and follows your iPhone’s language. To choose a different one for WatchWallet only, open Settings → Language in WatchWallet, which takes you to WatchWallet’s page in iOS Settings.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for WatchWallet in iOS Settings → Notifications → WatchWallet. Reminders are scheduled when you open WatchWallet; if you haven’t opened it for two weeks, open it once to schedule them again.

Automatic Capture

Automatic Capture uses an Apple Shortcuts automation that you set up for Apple Pay payments. WatchWallet can’t read Apple Wallet or your bank, and the Transaction trigger isn’t offered for every bank or region. You can always add expenses by hand, scan receipts or import a statement.
