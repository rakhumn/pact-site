Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

  • Your expenses, cards, receipts and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • WatchWallet has no account, no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • We never see, collect, sell or share your personal or financial data.

This policy explains what the WatchWallet iOS app (“WatchWallet”, “we”) does with information. WatchWallet is an expense tracker: it is not a bank and it does not move money.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you put into WatchWallet stays in the app’s storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection:

  • expenses, refunds and transfers (amounts, currencies, merchants, notes, dates, categories);
  • cards you add (a name, bank, payment network and only the last four digits — never full card numbers or security codes) and balances you enter;
  • receipts and bank confirmations you scan, import or share to WatchWallet, the text read from them, and your corrections;
  • subscriptions you track, products and price history, and your settings.

When you add a photo or PDF, WatchWallet removes its metadata (such as location) before saving it. Text is read from receipts with Apple’s on-device text recognition; nothing is uploaded.

2. Automatic Capture and sharing to WatchWallet

If you set up an Apple Shortcuts automation, iOS passes payment details (amount, merchant, card name) directly to WatchWallet on your iPhone. Items you share to WatchWallet from other apps are handed over on your iPhone as well. WatchWallet cannot read Apple Wallet, your bank or your transaction history.

3. Face ID and App Lock

App Lock uses Apple’s Local Authentication. Face ID, Touch ID and your passcode are checked by iOS; WatchWallet only learns whether unlocking succeeded. WatchWallet never receives or stores biometric data.

4. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone; there are no push notifications or servers. Reminders can show amounts and subscription names on the lock screen. While App Lock is on, they use general wording without amounts or names. You can turn reminders off in WatchWallet or in iOS Settings.

5. Purchases (WatchWallet Pro)

WatchWallet Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. WatchWallet checks on your iPhone whether you have an active purchase. Apple may give the developer aggregated sales reports that don’t identify you. Apple’s handling of purchases is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Exchange rates

With WatchWallet Pro’s currency conversion, WatchWallet downloads a public table of daily exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) at most about twice a day. The request is the same for everyone: it contains no personal or financial data, no identifiers and no cookies. As with any internet connection, the provider’s servers receive your device’s IP address; see exchangerate-api.com. You can turn this off in Settings → Exchange Rates.

7. When data leaves your device

Only through actions you choose:

  • Export: a CSV file you share using the iOS share sheet goes where you send it.
  • Backups: WatchWallet’s data is included in your iPhone and iCloud backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy terms.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual records, use Settings → Erase All Data, or delete the app. Because we don’t have your data, we can’t view, export, change or delete it for you, and there is nothing for us to disclose to third parties.

9. Children

WatchWallet is not directed to children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
